## Script issue and correction

The Pi copy at `/home/hackphs/software/deploy/pi-preflight.sh` had CRLF line endings. `bash -n` reproduced the reported syntax error at `case "$model" in` (`$'in\r'`). The script was backed up as `/home/hackphs/software/deploy/pi-preflight.sh.bak-20261008-231429`; trailing carriage returns were removed from the working copy. The other five Pi deployment scripts also had CRLF; they were backed up with suffix `.bak-crlf-20261008-232038` and normalized. All Pi deployment `.sh` files passed `bash -n`. The repository now has `.gitattributes` rule `*.sh text eol=lf`, and all six current Windows working copies under `software/deploy/` are LF (`git ls-files --eol` reports `w/lf`).

## Result

After the line-ending correction, `bash -n /home/hackphs/software/deploy/pi-preflight.sh` passed and `bash /home/hackphs/software/deploy/pi-preflight.sh` reported `Preflight passed.` Board, architecture, OS, Python, and memory checks passed.
