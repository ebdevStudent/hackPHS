FINAL BOM CORRECTIONS:
Batteries: 4 minimum; 5–6 preferred.
Battery test set: one light baseline, one mid-capacity pack, and one 750–850 mAh high-energy-density 2S pack.
Add: compatible LiHV charger, RP2040/Arduino bridge, exact trainer/serial cable, bearings/pivot hardware, gimbal-ring material, center-mount hardware, and VTX antenna if needed.
Spares: props, 1.0 mm + 1.5 mm rods, hubs, fasteners, heat-shrink, tape, and zip ties.
Optional only after the core works: RGB status LED, buzzer, switchable inspection LEDs.
Before ordering, verify the checkout delivery date; LiPo shipping remains the scheduled risk.
